About Us

XL-OODLES SERVICES LIMITED is a registered Facility and Property Maintenance Service Company in Lagos, Nigeria. The company is a self-motivated, flexible service provider, whose function is to manage/oversee an operating facility, provide solution to property, machinery and all its required systems to function efficiently, safely, securely and reliably for home and workplace in a manner consistent with prevailing regulations and standards.
