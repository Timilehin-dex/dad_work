About Us

Who We Are

SOYEMI-ODE AND COMPANY is a foremost professional firm registered to carry on the practice of Estate Surveying and Valuation, Facility Maintenance and Property Management, with commencement of full professional practice in April 2000. For over two decades, we have provided bespoke real estate services to a diversified clientele including financial institutions, oil & gas companies, government agencies, corporate organizations and high-net-worth individuals. Our practice is anchored on professionalism, integrity, due diligence and value creation, ensuring that every mandate is delivered with precision, confidentiality and regulatory compliance.

A registered member of the Estate Surveyors and Valuers Registration Board of Nigeria (ESVARBON) and the Nigerian Institution of Estate Surveyors and Valuers (NIESV), Fellow of Facility Management Professional (FMP) and holder of an MBA, he has led the firm from inception in April 2000 to become a reputable multi-branch practice serving clients across Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, FCT and Akwa Ibom.
